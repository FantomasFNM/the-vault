Document 01 — Node Operations
WHAT THE NODE IS

A Node is not a machine. It is not technology in any sense the human mind would recognize. It is older than the concept of technology. It predates every civilization that has risen and fallen on this planet. It predates the planet's current form.

A Node is a stabilization mechanism — a point of resonance between the surface world, the subterranean installations, and the larger fabric of what holds everything together. The simplest description available in human language: a Node is a knot. It holds the rope from unraveling.

There are 13 confirmed Node installations currently active beneath the surface of this world. Each one anchors a region. Each one connects to the others. Together they form a network that has kept this planet's reality — its physical laws, its continuity of consciousness, its capacity for life — stable across thousands of human generations.

The Covenant did not build the Nodes. The Covenant found them. What built them has not been identified. This remains the only question the Covenant has been unable to answer in recorded history.

The Nodes are fueled by collective human consciousness — specifically by what humans generate when they choose connection over isolation. Love. Music. Community. Shared grief. Collective purpose. These are not metaphors. They are measurable energy inputs that sustain Node stability across the network.

The inverse is also true. Fear. Hatred. Coordinated violence. Ontological shock at scale. These weaken the network. A sufficiently destabilized Node can cascade. The Covenant has observed this process on other worlds. It does not end well.

Document 03 — Anchor Designation Protocol
THE ANCHORS

Anchors are not chosen at random. They are cultivated across decades. An Anchor is a human whose psychological and emotional architecture makes them capable of holding resonance across a group — keeping a network of people connected, stable, and oriented toward positive consciousness output even under conditions of extreme stress or ontological disruption.

Anchors do not command. They do not lead in any conventional sense. They hold. When everything around them begins to fracture — relationships, reality, the fabric of what people believe to be true — an Anchor is the reason the fracture does not become a collapse.

There are multiple Anchors currently active on the surface. Each one serves a different network. Each network feeds into the Node closest to their primary location. The Anchors do not know each other. They do not know what they are. This is by design. An Anchor who understands their role too early becomes self-conscious. A self-conscious Anchor is less effective.

The Wyoming Anchor has been activated. The breach event accelerated a process that was already underway. His network is intact. His designation is confirmed. What he does with this knowledge is, as always, the variable the Covenant cannot predict. This is why they study. This is why Limbo exists.
